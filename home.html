Disease Awareness

Malaria

Symptoms

  • Fever and chills
  • Headache and muscle aches
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Sweating and shaking

Causes

  • Bite from infected Anopheles mosquito
  • Transmission through blood (rare)
  • Mother to unborn child (congenital)

Prevention

  • Use insecticide-treated mosquito nets
  • Apply mosquito repellent on skin
  • Eliminate standing water around homes
  • Take antimalarial drugs when traveling to high-risk areas
  • Wear long-sleeved clothing in evenings

Disease Awareness

Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

Symptoms

  • Often no symptoms (silent killer)
  • Headaches, especially in the morning
  • Shortness of breath
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Chest pain and fatigue

Causes

  • High salt intake in diet
  • Lack of physical activity
  • Obesity and overweight
  • Smoking and excessive alcohol
  • Stress and genetic factors

Prevention

  • Reduce salt and processed food consumption
  • Exercise regularly (30 minutes daily)
  • Maintain healthy weight
  • Limit alcohol and avoid smoking
  • Manage stress through relaxation techniques

Disease Awareness

Typhoid Fever

Symptoms

  • High fever that increases gradually
  • Headache and body weakness
  • Stomach pain and constipation or diarrhea
  • Loss of appetite and weight loss
  • Rose-colored spots on chest

Causes

  • Salmonella Typhi bacteria
  • Contaminated food or water
  • Poor sanitation and hygiene
  • Contact with infected person

Prevention

  • Drink safe, boiled or treated water
  • Practice proper handwashing with soap
  • Avoid raw fruits and vegetables that can't be peeled
  • Get vaccinated if in high-risk areas
  • Ensure proper food handling and cooking

Health Studies: Diseases in Northern Uganda

Learn about common diseases in our region, their symptoms and prevention

Malaria

Prevalence: Highly endemic in Northern Uganda, especially during rainy seasons (March-June, September-November).

Key Symptoms

  • High fever with chills and sweating
  • Headache and body aches
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • In severe cases: confusion, seizures, anemia

Prevention Strategies

  • Sleep under insecticide-treated mosquito nets (ITNs)
  • Use mosquito repellents and wear protective clothing
  • Clear stagnant water around homes
  • Take preventive medication if pregnant or traveling to high-risk areas
  • Seek prompt diagnosis and treatment

Typhoid Fever

Prevalence: Common in areas with poor sanitation and contaminated water sources.

Key Symptoms

  • Prolonged high fever
  • Stomach pain and discomfort
  • Headache and weakness
  • Loss of appetite and weight loss
  • Rose-colored spots on chest and abdomen

Prevention Strategies

  • Drink boiled or treated water
  • Practice proper handwashing with soap
  • Ensure proper food handling and cooking
  • Get vaccinated if in high-risk areas
  • Improve sanitation facilities

Hypertension

Prevalence: Increasing in urban areas due to lifestyle changes and high salt intake.

Key Symptoms

  • Often asymptomatic (silent condition)
  • Headaches, especially in the morning
  • Shortness of breath
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Chest pain and vision problems in severe cases

Prevention Strategies

  • Reduce salt intake in cooking and processed foods
  • Engage in regular physical activity (30 minutes daily)
  • Maintain healthy weight
  • Limit alcohol and avoid smoking
  • Regular blood pressure check-ups

Other Common Diseases

Respiratory Infections

  • Pneumonia: Common in children and elderly, especially during cold seasons
  • Tuberculosis: Still prevalent in crowded settings

Waterborne Diseases

  • Cholera: Occurs during floods and in areas with poor sanitation
  • Dysentery: Common in children, causes severe diarrhea

Prevention for All Diseases

  • Improve personal and environmental hygiene
  • Access to clean water and proper sanitation
  • Vaccination according to national schedule
  • Early diagnosis and treatment
  • Community health education
